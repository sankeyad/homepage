Alyssa Sankey         cv

  • I am a Teaching Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of New Brunswick. I teach calculus and linear algebra, combinatorial theory, graph theory, number theory, and a few other courses on occasion. My research interests are association schemes and coherent configurations, algebraic graph theory, design theory, and (very broadly) algebraic combinatorics.
