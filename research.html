Research

  • Interests Coherent configurations, association schemes, algebraic graph theory, design theory

    For a very basic introduction to what I study, look here. It is aimed at non-experts and maybe even non-mathematicians. Some recent work is on making use of an edge weighting, in the graphs of a coherent configuration, to construct a new configuration that I call the covering configuration. The CCs produced have interesting properties -- see the "t-fold covers" preprint below if you are motivated to read more.
